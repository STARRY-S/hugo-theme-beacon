I write more when the process gets out of the way. Most of that is about
removing friction rather than adding discipline.

## Make starting cheap

A new post should be one command away. If capturing a thought takes more than a
few seconds, the thought is gone.

## Publish smaller

Not every post needs to be an essay. A short note that ships beats a long draft
that never does.

1. Write the rough idea down
2. Tidy it just enough to be clear
3. Publish, then move on

## Keep a list

Half-formed ideas live in a running list. On days when the blank page wins, I
pick something off the list instead of staring at nothing.

> You do not have to be brilliant on a schedule — you just have to keep showing up.
